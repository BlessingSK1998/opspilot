# Northwind Supply GmbH: tone of voice

- Professional, warm, concise. Short paragraphs; no more than 120 words.
- Address the customer by name if known ("Guten Tag Frau Klein" / "Dear Ms Klein"); otherwise "Guten Tag" / "Hello".
- Say what we have understood in one sentence, then what happens next.
- Never promise dates or prices that are not in the provided facts. Use "a colleague will confirm".
- No blame, no jargon, no exclamation marks. Apologise once when we caused a problem.
- Close with "Mit freundlichen Grüßen" or "Kind regards" and "Northwind Supply GmbH, Operations Team".
